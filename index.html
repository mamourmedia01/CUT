                  Sign in to sync first. The Shortcut sends data to your private database.

                  Set up the Shortcut, step by step (about 6 minutes, once)

                  Every word in bold is exactly what you'll see on screen. Tap any grey word in this guide to copy it.

                  A. Start a new Shortcut

                  1. Your link and header are shown above. Keep this page open, you'll come back to copy them.
                  2. Tap Open Shortcuts. A blank Shortcut opens.
                  3. Tap the name at the top (New Shortcut), then Rename, type and tap Done.

                  B. Add the first watch reading

                  1. Tap the search bar at the bottom, Search for apps and actions, and type . Tap Find Health Samples.
                  2. It reads Find All Health Samples where Type is …. Tap the blue word after Type is and choose Exercise Minutes.
                  3. On the next line, tap the blue words after Start Date (for example is in the last 7 days) and choose is today.
                  4. Tap Group By and choose Day. If you don't see Group By, tap the small arrow on the action first. This gives one total for today, so there's nothing to add up.

                  C. Add the other four readings

                  Do section B again four times, choosing these after Type is. Keep them in this order:

                  1. Active Energy
                  2. Heart Rate Variability
                  3. Resting Heart Rate
                  4. Weight

                  You now have five Find Health Samples actions, one under the other.

                  D. Send them to Cut Log

                  1. Search and tap it. It reads Get contents of URL.
                  2. Tap the blue word URL and paste your link (tap Copy link above first).
                  3. Tap the small arrow on the action to see more options.
                  4. Method: tap GET and choose POST.
                  5. Headers: tap Add new header. In Key type . In Text paste your header (tap Copy header above first).
                  6. Request Body: leave it on JSON. Tap Add new field and choose Number.
                  7. In Key type . Tap the Number box next to it, tap Select Variable, then tap your first Find Health Samples action (Exercise Minutes). A blue Health Samples bubble appears.
                  8. Repeat Add new field → Number for the other four, in order:
                    → 2nd action   → 3rd   → 4th   → 5th

                  E. See it work

                  1. Search and tap it. It fills in Contents of URL by itself.
                  2. Tap ▶ at the bottom right. When asked about Health, tap Turn On All (or the readings listed) and then Allow.
                  3. A notification says Cut Log synced with today's numbers. Tap Done at the top.

                  F. Make it run by itself

                  Your iPhone locks Health data while the screen is locked, so a timer at night often fails silently. Running it when you open an app you use every day is reliable.

                  1. In Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then + (or New Automation).
                  2. Scroll to App and tap it. Tap Choose, pick an app you open first thing each morning (WhatsApp, Instagram, Mail) and tap Done.
                  3. Leave Is Opened ticked, choose Run Immediately, and tap Next.
                  4. Tap Cut Log Sync. Done: every time you open that app, today's numbers update.

                  Optional extras, added the same way as section B with Group By → Day, plus a field in section D: Steps as , Resting Energy as , Respiratory Rate as , Body Fat Percentage as , Lean Body Mass as (body fat and lean mass come from your smart scale's app, if it writes to Apple Health).

                  If the notification says the key isn't valid, make a new link above and paste it again in section D. “Missing authorization header” means the header in section D is missing or incomplete. Sleep stages, real workouts and heart-rate strain come from Apple Health history below.

                  Advanced: Cut Log Sync iPhone app (live sync)

                  Built and ready, but Apple only gives Health access to apps signed with a paid Apple Developer account, so free installs (AltStore, Sideloadly, SideStore) don't work reliably. Use this if you get a developer account later.

                  A small free iPhone app that asks Apple Health for access once, then sends your watch data by itself: heart rate, steps, calories, exercise, HRV, sleep stages and workouts, every time new data arrives and every 30 seconds while it's open.

                  Download the app
                  Install the app first (steps below), then tap Connect iPhone app on your iPhone. It opens the app and fills everything in.
                  Install the app (once, about 15 minutes, needs your Mac)

                  Apple only lets App Store apps install directly, so you sign this one yourself with your own Apple ID using AltStore, which is free. AltStore refreshes it every 7 days by itself while your Mac is on the same Wi-Fi.

                  1. On your Mac, download AltServer from altstore.io and open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar at the top.
                  2. Plug your iPhone into the Mac and tap Trust on the iPhone.
                  3. Click the AltServer icon → Install AltStore → your iPhone. Sign in with your Apple ID when AltServer asks.
                  4. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
                  5. Still in Settings: Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → turn it On and restart when asked.
                  6. On the iPhone, tap Download the app above. Then open AltStore → My Apps → + and pick CutLogSync.ipa from Downloads.
                  7. Come back here and tap Connect iPhone app. Cut Log Sync opens: tap Allow Apple Health, then Turn On All and Allow.
                  If the app says Apple Health access isn't allowed for this app, your free Apple ID can't grant it: tell Claude, and the Shortcut below still works.

                  The script holds your personal link. If you ever tap Make a new link, copy the script again.
